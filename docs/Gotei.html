1st Division

Genryusai (Shigekuni) Yamamoto — the Captain-Commander of the Gotei 13, representing absolute authority and power. As the founder of the organization, he maintained order in the Soul Society for over a thousand years. Calm yet ruthless, he commanded unmatched respect and fear among allies and enemies alike. His Zanpakuto, Ryujin Jakka, is the most powerful fire-type sword, capable of incinerating everything in its path. When released into Bankai, Zanka no Tachi, its flames burn with the heat of the sun, erasing anything they touch. Yamamoto embodies discipline, honor, and the unyielding will of a true warrior. In his final battle against Yhwach, he unleashes his full power but is ultimately defeated and killed.
Shunsui Kyoraku — after Genryusai Yamamoto’s death, he became the Captain-Commander of the Gotei 13, representing strategy and authority. Calm and composed, he commands respect from allies and fear from enemies. As a master swordsman, he wields dual Zanpakuto, Katen Kyokotsu, capable of creating deadly illusions. His Bankai, Katen Kyokotsu: Karamatsu Shinju, allows him to manipulate reality and trap opponents in lethal scenarios. Kyoraku combines tactical genius with a relaxed demeanor, often surprising foes. He values loyalty, discipline, and the protection of his subordinates. In his final confrontations, he demonstrates both leadership and incredible skill, shaping the outcome of major battles. His presence emphasizes intellect, adaptability, and authority, leaving a lasting impression as the Soul Society’s new leader.
Sasakibe Chojiro — the loyal lieutenant and right-hand of Genryusai Yamamoto, embodying unwavering dedication and discipline. He wields his Zanpakuto with precision, supporting his captain in critical battles. Calm and composed, he commands respect from allies and inspires confidence on the battlefield. His strategic mind allows him to anticipate enemy actions and act decisively. Sasakibe values honor, obedience, and the protection of his comrades.
Nanao Ise — the intelligent and composed lieutenant of the 1st Division, serving directly under Captain-Commander Shunsui Kyoraku. Known for her sharp intellect and calm demeanor, she handles both administrative and battlefield duties with precision. Nanao wields her Zanpakuto with discipline, using spiritual energy and kido to outsmart her opponents. Her loyalty to Kyoraku is unshakable, reflecting years of trust and partnership. Despite her reserved nature, she shows immense courage and resolve in moments of crisis.

2nd Division

Soi Fon — the calm and determined Captain of the 2nd Division, leading the Onmitsukido with precision and authority. Trained in stealth and assassination, she moves with unmatched speed and lethal grace. Her Zanpakuto, Suzumebachi, delivers instant death after two precise strikes, embodying efficiency and control. Beneath her stern exterior lies deep loyalty and respect for her former mentor, Yoruichi Shihoin. Soi Fon’s mastery of hand-to-hand combat and tactical planning makes her a formidable leader. She holds her subordinates to the same exacting standards she sets for herself. Despite her cold nature, she values duty, honor, and the strength of silent resolve. Soi Fon represents the perfect balance between discipline, precision, and unwavering determination.
Yoruichi Shihoin is the former Captain of the 2nd Division of the Gotei 13 and former Commander of the Onmitsukido in the Soul Society. She hails from the noble Shihoin Clan and was the first female head of her family. Despite her noble birth, Yoruichi carries herself with freedom, wit, and a playful attitude, often operating independently. She is renowned for her exceptional speed, hand-to-hand combat mastery, and use of the Shunpo technique. Yoruichi also served as mentor to many of the strongest Shinigami, teaching them the arts of stealth and battle. After stepping down from command, she continued to assist her allies covertly, acting outside the formal ranks of the Soul Society.
Marechiyo Omaeda — the loyal lieutenant of the 2nd Division, serving faithfully under Captain Soi Fon. Though often appearing lazy and arrogant, he possesses sharp instincts and remarkable combat awareness. His Zanpakuto, Gegetsuburi, grants him immense striking power, capable of devastating opponents with a single swing. Behind his carefree demeanor lies true courage and loyalty to his captain and comrades. In moments of crisis, Omaeda proves his worth through resilience and unexpected bravery.

3rd Division

Rojuro Otoribashi — the charismatic and flamboyant Captain of the 3rd Division, known for his love of music and flair. He wields his Zanpakuto, Kinshara, with both elegance and deadly precision, blending artistry with combat skill. His Bankai, Kinshara Butodan, enhances his attacks and adds dramatic force to every strike. Rose’s creativity and tactical thinking allow him to adapt quickly in battle, often surprising his opponents. Despite his playful and theatrical personality, he is deeply loyal to the Soul Society and his subordinates. He inspires his team through both strength and charisma, earning their respect and trust. In crucial fights, Rose combines courage, agility, and intelligence to control the battlefield. His presence embodies a balance of style, skill, and unwavering leadership within the Gotei 13.
Gin Ichimaru — the enigmatic and cunning Captain of the 3rd Division, feared for his speed and deceptive nature. He wields his Zanpakuto, Shinso, which can extend to deadly lengths in the blink of an eye. His mastery of swordsmanship and tactical thinking makes him a dangerous and unpredictable opponent. Beneath his ever-present smile lies a mind calculating every move, often keeping both allies and enemies uncertain. Gin values efficiency and precision in combat, striking with lethal intent. Despite his cold demeanor, he is fiercely loyal to the goals he believes in. In the final events of the Soul Society arc, Gin Ichimaru, Sosuke Aizen and Kaname Tosen in betraying the Soul Society.
Izuru Kira — the loyal and introspective lieutenant of the 3rd Division in the Gotei 13, known for his reserved demeanor and sharp intellect. He initially served under Captain Rojuro Otoribashi, who later became a Visored. After Otoribashi's exile, Kira continued his duties under Captain Gin Ichimaru. Following the defeat of Sosuke Aizen, Otoribashi was reinstated as captain, and Kira resumed his position as his lieutenant. Kira wields his Zanpakuto, Wabisuke, which doubles the weight of anything it strikes, making it a formidable weapon in skilled hands. His calm and strategic approach to combat, combined with unwavering loyalty, makes him a reliable and steadfast member of the Gotei 13.

4th Division

Retsu Unohana is a mysterious and powerful figure, the captain of the 4th Division, and one of the strongest in her world. Her strength comes not only from combat mastery but also from her unique healing abilities. Her Bankai, Minadzuki, unleashes devastating power capable of instantly overwhelming opponents. In battle, Unohana demonstrates incredible speed and precision, striking enemies with remarkable efficiency. Her main weapon is a katana, which combines lethal force with precise control over her opponents. She can analyze any adversary and find their weaknesses within seconds. Ultimately, Unohana died while fighting Kenpachi, revealing her true power and self-sacrifice. She remains the embodiment of unmatched strength, wisdom, and perfect self-control.
Isane Kotetsu is the current captain of the 4th Division, taking command after the death of Retsu Unohana. She is renowned for her healing mastery and flawless control over life energy. In battle, Isane demonstrates precision and strategic thinking, using her abilities to support allies and neutralize enemies. Her strength combines both defensive and healing skills, making her an invaluable member of the team. She carefully analyzes every opponent and chooses the optimal strategy for victory. Under her leadership, the 4th Division remains one of the most reliable and powerful units.
Isane Kotetsu served as the lieutenant of the 4th Division, supporting Captain Retsu Unohana and ensuring the unit’s operations. She possessed outstanding healing skills and control over life energy, helping allies recover after battles. In combat, Isane acted strategically, protecting the team and neutralizing threats. Her analytical abilities made her an indispensable assistant to the captain. She also trained new members and maintained discipline within the division. Her role as lieutenant laid the foundation for her future leadership and respect within the 4th Division.
Kiyone Kotetsu is the younger sister of Isane Kotetsu and serves as the lieutenant of the 4th Division in the Gotei 13. Previously, she held the position of 3rd Seat in the 13th Division, sharing duties with Sentaro Kotsubaki. Kiyone is known for her openness, energy, and dedication to her duties. She actively supports her sister in her role as captain, providing effective leadership and support to the division. Her combat skills, including proficiency with her Zanpakuto and shunpo, make her a valuable ally in battle. Kiyone is also recognized for her sense of humor and efforts to maintain team morale.

5th Division

Shinji Hirako is the captain of the 5th Division in the Gotei 13 and the leader of the Visored group. He was one of eight Soul Reapers subjected to Sosuke Aizen's experiment to turn them into Hollows, leading to their exile from the Soul Society. Despite this, Shinji retained his humanity and became the leader of the Visoreds, a group dedicated to protecting the human world. His Zanpakuto, Sakanade, has the ability to invert the perception of space, causing opponents to attack in the opposite direction. Shinji also possesses a Hollow mask that enhances his physical abilities and spiritual energy. His leadership qualities, determination, and dedication to his cause make him one of the most respected characters in the series.
Sosuke Aizen was the captain of the 5th Division in the Gotei 13, with Momo Hinamori as his lieutenant. Before becoming captain, he served as the lieutenant under Shinji Hirako, who led the division at that time. Outwardly, he appeared calm and respected by his fellow Soul Reapers, but he concealed his true intentions. Aizen possessed a brilliant mind, capable of anticipating opponents’ actions and planning events years in advance. His intellect and analytical skills made him nearly unbeatable in any situation. Instead of serving the Soul Society, he sought power and immortality. Ultimately, Aizen betrayed the Soul Society alongside Gin Ichimaru and Kaname Tosen. Afterward, they began forming the Espada to achieve their ambitions. His betrayal shook the Soul Society and forever altered the balance of power.
Momo Hinamori is the lieutenant of the 5th Division in the Gotei 13, serving under Captain Shinji Hirako. She is known for her loyalty, kindness, and care for her allies. Momo possesses solid combat skills and can control spiritual energy to protect the team. In battle, she demonstrates courage and determination, supporting her captain and squad in difficult situations. Her devotion makes her a reliable assistant and an important member of the division. Momo combines bravery and kindness, making her indispensable to the 5th Division.

6th Division

Byakuya Kuchiki is the captain of the 6th Division in the Gotei 13 and the head of the Kuchiki Clan. He is known for his cold demeanor, composure, and exceptional combat skills. Byakuya wields his Zanpakuto, Senbonzakura, with incredible precision and speed, capable of overwhelming his opponents effortlessly. His strategic thinking and analytical abilities make him one of the most dangerous captains. Despite his cold exterior, he deeply values family honor and upholding traditions. In battle, Byakuya demonstrates patience and careful calculation, never losing control of the situation. He has participated in key battles against the strongest enemies of the Soul Society. His strength, experience, and determination make him a model for all Soul Reapers and a symbol of the 6th Division’s power.
Renji Abarai is the lieutenant of the 6th Division in the Gotei 13, serving under Captain Byakuya Kuchiki. He is known for his energy, hot-headed personality, and loyalty to his captain and friends. Renji possesses immense physical strength and mastery of his Zanpakuto, Zabimaru, which can change form for combat. His fighting spirit and perseverance make him a dangerous opponent. He is willing to risk his life for his allies and shows intelligence and strategic thinking in critical moments. Renji’s energy, strength, and loyalty make him an indispensable member of the 6th Division.

7th Division

Sajin Komamura is the captain of the 7th Division in the Gotei 13, known for his honesty, loyalty, and strong sense of duty. He possesses immense physical strength and mastery over his Zanpakuto, Sakkeko, which can grow to enormous sizes. During the TYBW arc, he fought against Bambietta Basterbine, losing his arm and receiving a cybernetic replacement. In search of power for vengeance, he used a technique that transformed him into a giant wolf, losing his ability to speak and his human form. Despite this, he continued to fight, but eventually, his body was destroyed, leaving only his Zanpakuto as a remnant.
Tetsuzaemon Iba became the captain of the 7th Division in the Gotei 13 after the events of the Thousand-Year Blood War (TYBW) and the disappearance of Sajin Komamura. He previously served as a lieutenant and proved himself as a loyal, disciplined, and reliable member of the division. His experience, combat skills, and strategic thinking made him a natural choice for promotion. Iba is known for his determination, loyalty to his comrades, and ability to lead the division in difficult situations. Under his command, the 7th Division has become more organized and effective in battle. He continues to protect the Soul Society and maintain order among the Soul Reapers.
Love Aikawa is the former captain of the 7th Division in the Gotei 13 and later becomes a member of the Visored. He is known for his calm, easygoing personality and strong sense of loyalty to his comrades. His Zanpakuto, Tengumaru, transforms into a massive spiked club that releases powerful fire-based attacks. After being Hollowfied by Aizen’s experiments, he was exiled from Soul Society and joined the Visoreds. During the Arrancar arc, he fought alongside them against Aizen’s army, showing great strength and determination despite his fall from captaincy.
Tetsuzaemon Iba was the lieutenant of the 7th Division in the Gotei 13 under Captain Sajin Komamura. He was known for his loyalty, discipline, and responsible approach to his duties. Iba was skilled in combat techniques and spiritual energy, supporting his captain and division in battles. In combat, he demonstrated strategic thinking, determination, and a willingness to protect his allies at any cost. After the events of the TYBW, his leadership qualities and experience led to him becoming the new captain of the 7th Division.
Atau Rindo is the lieutenant of the 7th Division in the Gotei 13, serving under Captain Tetsuzaemon Iba. He is a tall, fair-skinned young man with dark, shaggy hair and a mole beneath his right eye. Rindo wears a variation of the standard Shinigami uniform with shortened sleeves, a light-colored turtleneck, and a light-colored apron with large pockets. He is polite and has a great fondness for animals, often spending time in the division's garden. Due to his deafness, Rindo communicates entirely in sign language but has also trained himself to read lips, facilitating interactions with others. In combat, he is brutal and ruthless, delivering swift and gory deaths to his enemies without hesitation.

8th Division

Shunsui  Kyoraku served as Captain of the 8th Division of the Gotei 13, holding a distinguished position as a master swordsman and tactician. He wields two swords and is capable of switching strength between his hands, making him one of the most skilled Soul Reapers. By personality, Kyoraku is laid-back and prefers dialogue over conflict, yet stands ready to fight when required. During his tenure as captain of the 8th Division he participated in major battles and helped defend the Seireitei from formidable threats. Thanks to his leadership and battlefield achievements he was later promoted to Captain of the 1st Division and ultimately became the Captain-Commander of the Gotei 13.
Lisa Yadomaru is the Captain of the 8th Division of the Gotei 13. Formerly serving as its lieutenant under Captain Shunsui Kyoraku, she assumed command after his promotion. Lisa is calm, straightforward, and highly disciplined, earning respect from her subordinates as a capable and focused leader. She wields a powerful Zanpakuto and demonstrates great skill in close combat and kido, enhanced by her experience as a Visored. Despite her reserved demeanor, she is assertive and decisive in battle. Her return to the Gotei 13 symbolizes reconciliation between the Soul Reapers and the Visoreds.
Nanao Ise served as the Lieutenant of the 8th Division of the Gotei 13 under Captain Shunsui Kyoraku. She was known for her exceptional skill in kido and barrier creation, marking her as one of the most capable Soul Reapers of her rank. Serious and disciplined, she handled administrative duties and maintained order within the division. Despite her captain’s carefree attitude, she remained loyal and balanced his personality. In battle, Nanao showed determination and skillful use of defensive spells. She was later promoted to Lieutenant of the 1st Division of the Gotei 13.
Yuyu Yayahara serves as the Lieutenant of the 8th Division of the Gotei 13 under Captain Lisa Yadomaru. She stands out with tanned skin and long platinum hair tipped in dark. In combat, she uses the “Ursine Manifestation,” summoning a giant bear head capable of decapitating an opponent in one bite. Her style blends speed, strength, and surprise, shown in her battle against the Jigoku no Gaki. Despite her youth, Yuyu confidently fulfills her lieutenant duties.
Lisa Yadomaru is the former lieutenant of the 8th Division under Shunsui Kyoraku and later becomes a member of the Visored. She is calm, intelligent, and disciplined, often serving as the voice of reason among her comrades. Her Zanpakuto, Haguro Tonbo, grants her strong melee abilities, though its full power remains unknown. After being Hollowfied and exiled from Soul Society, she eventually returns and becomes the captain of the 8th Division after the Thousand-Year Blood War.

9th Division

Kensei Muguruma is the Captain of the 9th Division of the Gotei 13, reinstated after the events with Aizen. He was formerly one of the Visored, gaining Hollow powers through Aizen’s experiments. His Zanpakuto, Tachikaze, in Shikai becomes a short blade with wind explosions, while his Bankai, Tekken Tachikaze, amplifies his strength and destructive impact. Kensei is strict and hot-tempered but cares for his subordinates and acts decisively in battle. During the Quincy invasion in the “Thousand-Year Blood War” arc, he once again led the 9th Division and fought Mask de Masculine.
Kaname Tosen was the captain of the 9th Division of the Gotei 13, known for his pursuit of justice and pacifist ideals. However, disillusioned with Soul Society, he betrayed it by joining Sosuke Aizen and Gin Ichimaru. Tosen betrayed his division by killing Kensei Muguruma and other members, then defected from Soul Society. He became one of the leaders of Aizen's Arrancar army and was transformed into a Hollow, gaining abilities surpassing those of ordinary Shinigami. His actions led to conflicts with former allies, including Sajin Komamura and Shuhei Hisagi. Tosen played a pivotal role in the formation of the Espada, becoming one of the most formidable adversaries of the Gotei 13.
Shuhei Hisagi is the Lieutenant of the 9th Division of the Gotei 13. He initially served under Captain Kaname Tosen before Tosen’s betrayal and alliance with Aizen and Gin. Afterward, Hisagi became lieutenant under Captain Kensei Muguruma, continuing to maintain order and protect the division’s members. His Zanpakuto, Kazeshini, in Shikai form takes the shape of twin sickles connected by a chain, allowing him to attack at range and use lightning techniques. Hisagi is known for his calm, disciplined demeanor and strong sense of justice. He shows decisiveness in battle and upholds discipline in the division, remaining loyal to his duties.
Mashiro Kuna is the former lieutenant of the 9th Division under Kensei Muguruma and later becomes a member of the Visored. She is known for her childish, energetic personality and tendency to act impulsively in battle. After being Hollowfied by Aizen’s experiments, she joined the other exiled Shinigami. Mashiro specializes in powerful kicks and hand-to-hand combat, and when using her Hollow mask, her strength, speed, and endurance increase dramatically. During the Thousand-Year Blood War, she fights alongside Kensei and the Visoreds against the Wandenreich, showing her loyalty and courage despite her playful nature.

10th Division

Toshiro Hitsugaya is the Captain of the 10th Division of the Gotei 13 and the youngest captain in Soul Society’s history. He entered the Shinigami Academy at age nine and graduated in just one year, displaying remarkable talent. After serving under Isshin Shiba, Hitsugaya became captain, with Rangiku Matsumoto as his lieutenant. His Zanpakuto, Hyorinmaru, controls ice and water, and in Bankai form, Daiguren Hyorinmaru, grants him ice wings and a dragon that enhances both defense and attack power. Toshiro is known for his seriousness, composure, and strong sense of duty toward Soul Society. He played a key role in the Soul Society and Thousand-Year Blood War arcs, showing great maturity and leadership.
Isshin Kurosaki was the former Captain of the 10th Division of the Gotei 13 before the main events of Bleach. He served as captain with Rangiku Matsumoto as his lieutenant and Toshiro Hitsugaya as a seated officer under his command. Isshin wielded the Zanpakuto Engetsu, which allowed him to use flame-based techniques and fight with considerable power. Unlike many captains, he was known for his carefree, informal personality and relaxed attitude toward discipline. He left Soul Society after losing his powers while saving Masaki Kurosaki and began living in the Human World. Years later, he became the father of Ichigo Kurosaki, who was born afterward and inherited exceptional spiritual potential.
Rangiku Matsumoto is the Lieutenant of the 10th Division of the Gotei 13 under Captain Toshiro Hitsugaya. Her Zanpakuto, Haineko, in its Shikai form turns into a cloud of ash that she manipulates for both attack and defense. Despite her carefree demeanor, Rangiku takes her duties seriously and shows determination in battle. She was raised in Rukongai, where Gin Ichimaru saved her life, forming a deep yet complicated bond between them. During Aizen’s rebellion, she fought to protect her allies and showed resilience even after being severely wounded.

11th Division

Kenpachi Zaraki is the Captain of the 11th Division in the Gotei 13, famed for his love of combat and ruthless fighting style. He was raised in Rukongai District 80 and adopted the surname “Zaraki” from the area where he lived. He claimed the title “Kenpachi” by defeating the previous holder, becoming captain despite never fully awakening his Zanpakuto’s name. His Zanpakuto, Nozarashi, is wielded through immense spiritual pressure, and he often restrains himself so that fights last longer. Zaraki wears an eyepatch that partially seals off his reiatsu and bells in his hair to allow enemies to sense his movement. Despite his ferocity, he respects and protects his subordinates, especially Yachiru. In the “Thousand-Year Blood War” arc he battles powerful Quincies, showcasing immense endurance and continued development.
Yachiru Kusajishi is the Lieutenant of the 11th Division of the Gotei 13 under Kenpachi Zaraki. She appears as a small, cheerful girl but hides immense combat potential. Her Zanpakuto, Sanpo Kenju, creates two invisible copies of her attacks, increasing their impact. Yachiru can emit vast spiritual energy and can even lift Zaraki despite the size difference. It is later revealed that she is a manifestation of Kenpachi’s Bankai and disappears once he learns his Zanpakuto’s true name. Afterward, her lieutenant position is filled by Ikkaku Madarame.
Ikkaku Madarame is the Lieutenant of the 11th Division of the Gotei 13 under Captain Kenpachi Zaraki. He previously served as the 3rd Seat and is from Rukongai, where he sought strong opponents from a young age. His Zanpakuto, Hozukimaru, in Shikai transforms into a long spear with a rotating blade, while in Bankai it grows in size and power. Despite his love of combat, he keeps his Bankai hidden to avoid drawing attention to his true strength. In battle, he prefers using Shikai and skillful techniques, valuing mastery and strategy. His loyalty to his captain and fighting spirit make Ikkaku a reliable and valuable member of the division.

12th Division

Mayuri Kurotsuchi is the Captain of the 12th Division of the Gotei 13 and head of the Shinigami Research and Development Institute. He previously served as the 3rd Seat and Vice-President of the institute under Kisuke Urahara. After Urahara was banished from the Seireitei, Mayuri requested to become captain and took command of the 12th Division. He is known for his sadistic personality, lack of empathy, and willingness to use living beings in experiments. His Zanpakuto, Ashisogi Jizo, creates a giant poisonous caterpillar that can disperse toxins and control their concentration. Mayuri also conducts biotechnological research, including the creation of his artificial daughter Nemu Kurotsuchi and modifications to his own body.
Kisuke Urahara is the former captain of the 12th Division in the Gotei 13 and the founder of the Shinigami Research and Development Institute. He is known for his eccentric yet intelligent personality and his mastery of science, strategy, and combat. His Zanpakuto, Benihime, can release destructive energy blasts, create defensive barriers, and manipulate spiritual energy with great precision. His Bankai, Kannonbiraki Benihime Aratame, manifests as a giant feminine figure that can restructure anything it touches — including flesh, objects, and the battlefield itself. This power allows Urahara to heal injuries, alter his surroundings, or dismantle opponents on a cellular level. After being exiled from Soul Society for forbidden research, he settled in the human world and opened the Urahara Shop, continuing to assist the Shinigami from the shadows.
Nemu Kurotsuchi is the Lieutenant of the 12th Division of the Gotei 13 under Mayuri Kurotsuchi. She is an artificial soul created by Mayuri using Gigai and Gikon technologies. Despite her origins, Nemu is completely loyal to her “father” and is willing to put herself in danger to protect him. Her personality is shy and introverted, and she rarely shows her emotions. In combat, Nemu uses a unique technique, spinning her arm like a drill to pierce objects.
Hiyori Sarugaki is the former lieutenant of the 12th Division under Kisuke Urahara and later becomes a member of the Visored. She is known for her short temper, blunt speech, and fierce loyalty to her comrades. Her Zanpakuto, Kubikiri Orochi, transforms into a large cleaver-like blade suited for aggressive close combat. After being Hollowfied by Aizen’s experiments, she gained enhanced strength and speed, as well as the ability to fire a Cero using her Hollow mask. Although she aided the Shinigami during the Thousand-Year Blood War, she never returned to her post and remains a Visored.

13th Division

Jushiro Ukitake is the Captain of the 13th Division of the Gotei 13 and one of the oldest Shinigami in the Seireitei. From a young age, he suffered from a deadly lung disease but was saved by a being called Mimihagi, which replaced his lungs and allowed him to survive. Despite this, his health remained fragile. Ukitake was known for his kindness, wisdom, and sense of justice, always ready to help his subordinates and friends. He was a close friend of Shunsui Kyoraku, with whom he studied at the Shinigami Academy and later became captains together. In battle, Jushiro demonstrated a high level of skill, though his condition sometimes prevented him from fully realizing his potential. During the Thousand-Year Blood War, he performed the Kamikake ritual, but after Yhwach absorbed Mimihagi, Ukitake died, sacrificing his life for Soul Society.
Rukia Kuchiki is the Lieutenant of the 13th Division of the Gotei 13 under Captain Jushiro Ukitake. She was born into the noble Kuchiki clan but grew up in Rukongai after a tragedy in her district. Rukia is known for her calm and composed demeanor, yet she shows determination and skill in battle. Her Zanpakuto, Sode no Shirayuki, is considered one of the most beautiful and powerful ice-type swords among Shinigami, allowing her to freeze opponents and the surrounding environment. She trained under and assisted her captain, demonstrating loyalty and responsibility. Rukia played a key role in the battle against Aizen and subsequent events, showcasing strategic thinking and strong spirit.
