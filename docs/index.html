Ichigo Kurosaki
Ichigo Kurosaki is the main protagonist of Bleach and a high school student from Karakura Town who becomes a Substitute Soul Reaper after meeting Rukia Kuchiki. Over time, it is revealed that he possesses powers of a Shinigami, Hollow, and Quincy, making him unique among all spiritual beings. His Zanpakuto, Zangetsu, grants him immense strength and speed, especially in his Bankai form, Tensa Zangetsu. Ichigo is brave and selfless, always fighting to protect his friends and maintain balance between worlds. He plays a central role in major battles against Aizen, the Arrancar, and the Wandenreich. After the wars, Ichigo returns to a peaceful life in Karakura while remaining a guardian of both the Human World and the Soul Society.
